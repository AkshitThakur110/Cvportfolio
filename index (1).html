A bit about how I build

Currently in my first year of a B.Tech in AI & ML, spending most of my time moving concepts out of the classroom and into working code.

I'm an Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning undergraduate at Lovely Professional University, Phagwara. My interest sits at the point where a model actually has to work with messy, real input — a webcam feed, a raw dataset, an inbox full of spam — rather than a clean textbook example.

That shows in the range of what I've shipped so far: a real-time computer vision system built with CNNs and OpenCV, a recommendation engine built from scratch on a multi-thousand-title dataset, a normalized MySQL system for managing student records, and an NLP classifier evaluated across multiple models rather than settled on the first one that worked.

Outside of project work, I've been logging consistent reps on core fundamentals — Python, C, and database design — and backing that up with certifications and 200+ solved problems.
